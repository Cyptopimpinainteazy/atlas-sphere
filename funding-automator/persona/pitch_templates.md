# Pitch Templates — Micro Pitches and Email Variants

Use these templates as the base prompts or direct copy for outreach. They are intentionally short and adaptable by the generator.

VC / Investor micro-pitch (Variant A — data-first):

Subject: 15-min demo — dual-VM chain with reproducible TPS

Hi {name},

We built a dual-VM chain combining EVM compatibility with SVM parallelization. Real testnet benchmarks show reproducible TPS improvements and micro-fees (~$0.001 per tx). We’ve released a public testnet, explorer, and two demo dApps that show the UX and performance improvements.

3 quick traction bullets:
- working testnet + faucet (dev onboarding < 30s)
- public explorer + reproducible benchmark harness
- 2 demo dApps + early integrations

Would you have 15 minutes for a demo next week? — NovaFlux / team
